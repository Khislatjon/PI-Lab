Work with us

We take on PhD researchers, postdocs and industrial partners working at the intersection of imaging, mechanics and machine learning. Bring a specimen, a dataset or a question.

Openings

Ways in

Formal positions and informal collaboration both work.

PhD researchers

We supervise doctoral work across tissue mechanics, hyperspectral imaging, biomaterial design and quantum representation. Applications are welcome year-round; funded studentships are advertised as they open.

Enquire with a CV and a short research statement

Postdocs and fellows

We support fellowship applications where the research fits the lab's direction. Get in touch early — proposals are stronger when the imaging plan is built in from the start.

Contact before the funder deadline

Industrial partners

Knowledge Transfer Partnerships, contract imaging and joint projects. Our KTP on defect identification in bio-composites is one model for how this works.

See KTP-10108115 on the projects page

Affiliates

Bio‑AImagiQ is deliberately open: researchers we do not line-manage can contribute to and benefit from the initiative. If you work in an adjacent area, there is room here.
